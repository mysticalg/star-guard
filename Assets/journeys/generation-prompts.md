## giant-hulls-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game sprite sheet for an original top-down vertical arcade shooter Star Guard. Six enormous finale boss hulls in a strict 3 columns x 2 rows equal-cell grid, each isolated with generous transparent gutters, no labels or text. Orthographic directly overhead, nose/front towards bottom, bilateral silhouettes, richly shaded 1990s pre-rendered metal and organic arcade sprites, sharp readable plating and jewel-like glowing cores. Match intricate shaded metallic arcade spacecraft style, not pixel blocks or flat vector. Row 1: storm-coast ocean salvage leviathan with steel teal hull and orange turbines; hive queen with amber chitin, violet abdomen and hooked limbs; shattered-belt crystal dreadnought with irregular violet mineral armour and silver machinery. Row 2: frozen citadel with icy blue armour and red furnace vents; living-nebula beast heart with original alien dark magenta fleshy rib armour and green acid sacs; reactor-run giant industrial colossus with charcoal armour, orange reactor coils and massive central death-ray aperture. Wide screen-filling designs substantially broader than ordinary fighter ships. Every hull has clearly separated visual sockets for two shield generators, two outer cannons and central reactor, leaving these sockets dark and empty so separate destructible components can overlay them in the game. No background, no stars, no scenery, no lettering, no shadows outside the objects. Actual transparent alpha.

The fifth hull was superseded by the worm request and is retained in the source sheet; the fifth fight renders the worm instead.
